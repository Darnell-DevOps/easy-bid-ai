# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

CloseSync AI is launching for freelancers, consultants, and small agencies that sell client services. These users manage their own or their team's client-closing process and need to respond to enquiries, qualify opportunities, create proposals, collect signatures and payments, and onboard new clients without losing context between tools.

The initial audience is intentionally narrower than all service businesses.

## Product Purpose

CloseSync AI helps freelancers, consultants, and small agencies turn enquiries into paying clients faster. It connects lead capture, AI-assisted qualification, proposal generation, contracts, payments, onboarding, kickoff, and ongoing client management in one workflow.

Success means that a service provider can move a prospective client from first enquiry into paid, organised delivery while keeping the history, decisions, documents, and next actions connected.

## Positioning

CloseSync AI is a client-closing workflow platform, not merely a proposal generator.

Its core mechanism is one continuous client record and workflow spanning enquiry, proposal, contract, payment, onboarding, kickoff, and ongoing work. AI assists within that connected operating context rather than acting as a separate novelty feature.

## Operating Context

Users work across client enquiries, lead qualification, proposals, contracts, payment collection, onboarding forms, kickoff arrangements, retainers, bookings, email communication, and ongoing client records. CloseSync AI brings those activities into a single operational flow while still supporting client-facing links and documents.

The launch is UK-first. The product should be able to support global users later, so future decisions should avoid unnecessary UK-only technical constraints unless they are required for the launch.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Lead capture and AI-assisted qualification.
- Proposal creation, approval, and client-facing proposal experiences.
- Contract creation and electronic signatures.
- Payment collection and recurring retainers through Paddle.
- Client onboarding, kickoff, booking, and ongoing client management.
- Email communication, templates, reminders, and scheduled workflow automation.
- Authenticated operational dashboards and token-protected client-facing flows.
- Hosted as a web application using Lovable Cloud with Supabase-backed data and Edge Functions.
- UK-first launch with future global support.

## Brand Commitments

- Official product name: **CloseSync AI**.
- Parent and umbrella brand: **StriveSync**.
- Product messaging must present CloseSync AI as a complete client-closing workflow platform, not only a proposal generator.
- AI should be communicated as useful assistance inside a credible workflow, not as a gimmick or unsupported promise.

## Evidence on Hand

The repository contains working product flows, a sample proposal, client-facing previews, automated tests, and implementation evidence for the capabilities listed above.

There are no confirmed customer testimonials, customer counts, case studies, performance benchmarks, or outcome statistics yet. Future work must not invent or imply this proof.

## Product Principles

1. Keep the complete client journey connected to one durable record.
2. Make the next action clear from enquiry through paid onboarding and ongoing work.
3. Use AI to reduce administrative effort while keeping important actions visible and controllable.
4. Earn trust through accurate product claims, dependable workflows, and professional client-facing experiences.
5. Serve the focused launch audience well before broadening the market position.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Future work should target WCAG 2.1 AA as the default quality standard. This is a product quality target, not a claim that the entire application has been formally certified or independently audited.
